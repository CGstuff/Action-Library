Applying

Getting animation out of the library and onto a rig. You drive this from the app — select the armature in Blender, then double-click a card.

The basics

  1. In Blender, select the armature you want to animate.
  2. In Action Library, double-click the card.

The animation is assigned to your rig as its active action. Blender doesn't need to be in any particular mode and you don't need to switch windows back and forth — the app tells Blender directly.

Modifiers

Hold a key while double-clicking to change what happens:

HoldDoes
CtrlMirror — flip left to right.
ShiftAs a slot — add as an action slot rather than replacing the action (actions only).
AltAt the playhead — insert here instead of replacing.
Ctrl+ShiftMirrored, as a slot.

The Apply panel on the right has the same options as checkboxes. Set them there and a plain double-click uses them — handy when you're applying lots of things the same way.

What the options do

Mirror

Flips the animation across the rig's axis of symmetry, so a left-hand wave becomes a right-hand wave. It relies on your rig's bones following a standard left/right naming convention (.L / .R and similar) — that's how Blender knows which bone pairs with which.

At the playhead

Instead of replacing the rig's action, the keyframes are inserted starting at the current frame, merging with what's already there. Use it to chain moves together — a walk, then a turn, then a stop.

Reverse

Plays the animation backwards, flipping the keyframes around the range.

Selected bones only

Applies only to the bones you currently have selected in Blender, leaving everything else untouched. Good for taking just the arms from one take.

Use action slots

Adds the animation as a slot on a layered action rather than replacing the rig's action, so several animations can sit on one rig at once. Needs Blender 4.5 or newer.

Applying poses

Double-click a pose card and the pose is applied to your rig immediately. Ctrl mirrors it, exactly as with animations.

Pose blending

Poses can be dialled in rather than snapped to. Right-click and drag on a pose card:

  • Drag right to blend further toward the pose; an overlay shows the percentage.
  • Hold Ctrl while dragging to blend toward the mirrored pose.
  • Left-click or press Escape to cancel and put the rig back.
  • Release to keep the blend where you left it.

Your rig updates live in Blender as you drag, so you're judging the result by eye rather than by number.

Rig compatibility

Animations remember which rig type they were made for, and Action Library compares that against the armature you're applying to. If they don't match you'll be warned — the animation may still apply, but bones that don't exist on the target simply won't be driven.

Use the rig-type filter while browsing to only see animations that fit the rig you're working on.

If Blender isn't running

Applying works over a direct connection to Blender. If Blender isn't open, the app doesn't lose your request — it queues it, and it applies as soon as Blender is running with the addon enabled. The status bar tells you when that's happened.

When apply doesn't work

  • Nothing happens — is an armature selected in Blender? Applying needs a target.
  • "Cannot connect to Blender" — Blender must be running with the addon enabled, and the port must match on both sides. See install troubleshooting.
  • The animation applies but looks wrong — check the rig type matches. A rig with different bone names will take the parts that line up and ignore the rest.
  • Mirroring doesn't mirror — the rig's bones probably don't use a left/right naming convention Blender recognises.
